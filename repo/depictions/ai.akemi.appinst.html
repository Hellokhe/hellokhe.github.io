Overview

appinst is a lightweight command-line tool for installing .ipa application packages directly from MobileTerminal, NewTerm, or an SSH session on your iOS 7 device.

It acts as the official companion utility to AppSync Unified.

Usage Example

Transfer your IPA to your device, then in terminal run:

appinst /var/mobile/Downloads/MyGame.ipa

The application will be installed directly to your SpringBoard home screen!
